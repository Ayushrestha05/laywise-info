LayWise is a poultry farm management application that allows you to:

- Manage farm sheds and bird houses
- Track egg production and flock daily logs
- Record sales and expenses
- Manage inventory and feed production
- Track employee information and salary payments
- Generate reports and analytics based on the data you enter into the App

Note: "Reports and analytics" refers to summaries and insights generated from your own farm data within the App (for example, production or sales totals). It does not involve us collecting or analyzing data about your use of the App — see our Privacy Policy for details.
